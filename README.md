## The entry

> At midnight the fridge stopped humming, and the silence was so complete I could hear the house holding its breath.

One sentence, 114 characters. The wonder is not the fridge — it is the silence
the fridge was hiding. Every home has a hum you stop hearing until the moment
it stops, and in that gap the whole house suddenly feels alive, listening back.
The gate asks for 1–200 characters of UTF-8 text; this entry is 114, one
sentence, no trailing newline.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing is generated at
run time and nothing is read from the network: the sentence is fixed in the
source, so every run produces the same entry.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
